🛡️ Digital Safety Guide

Choose a topic to learn simple safety tips.

📱 Social Media

Protect privacyUse private account settings and avoid posting personal information.
Think before postingAsk: “Would I be comfortable if my family saw this?”

🎮 Online Games

Don't share personal detailsNever give strangers your real name, address, school, or password.
Use reporting toolsBlock and report players who threaten or harass you.

🚫 Cyberbullying

Don't fight backSave evidence, block the person, and tell a trusted adult.

🔗 Scams & Fake Links

Stop and checkDon't click suspicious links or give away passwords and OTPs.

🔐 Privacy & Passwords

Strong passwordsUse unique passwords and never share them with online strangers.

🚨 Help & Support

Someone is threatening you

Don't respond or meet the person. Save screenshots, block the account, and tell a trusted adult.

You clicked a suspicious link

Stop entering information. Change affected passwords and ask a trusted adult for help.

You're being cyberbullied

Keep evidence, block/report the account, and talk to a parent, teacher, or another trusted adult.
